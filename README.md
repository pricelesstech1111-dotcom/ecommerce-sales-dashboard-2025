# ecommerce-sales-dashboard-2025

  Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales data to evaluate business performance and identify trends across revenue, orders, products, categories, regions, and payment methods.

I developed an interactive sales dashboard using Google Sheets to transform raw sales data into clear and actionable business insights.

## Business Problem

The business generated a large volume of e-commerce sales data but needed a clear way to understand its sales performance. The data contained information about revenue, orders, products, regions, and payment methods.

The goal was to analyze the data and create a dashboard that provides a clear overview of business performance and supports data-driven decision-making.

## Objectives

* Analyze overall sales performance
* Track key performance indicators (KPIs)
* Identify sales trends over time
* Analyze product and category performance
* Compare regional sales performance
* Analyze customer payment methods
* Present findings through a visual dashboard

## Tools & Skills

The dashboard makes it easier to monitor business performance, identify sales patterns, and support data-driven decision-making.

## Recommendations

* Focus on high-performing products and maintain adequate stock.
* Strengthen categories that contribute significantly to revenue.
* Monitor regional performance to identify growth opportunities.
* Maintain multiple payment options to accommodate customer preferences.
* Track monthly revenue trends to improve sales planning.

The project strengthened my skills in data cleaning, analysis, visualization, KPI tracking, dashboard development, and communicating data-driven findings.
